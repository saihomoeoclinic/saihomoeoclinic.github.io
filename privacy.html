Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This website does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

This privacy policy applies to the website saihomoeoclinic.com.

2. What data we collect

This website collects no personal data directly. Specifically:

  • No registration or login required
  • No contact forms that store data on our servers
  • No cookies set by this website
  • No patient records stored online
  • No payment information collected

3. WhatsApp enquiry form

When you use the enquiry form on our website, your message is sent directly to the clinic's WhatsApp account via WhatsApp's own service. The information you enter — name, phone number, condition and preferred time — is transmitted through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

We do not store this information on any server. It exists only in the clinic's WhatsApp inbox.

4. Calendly booking

Appointment bookings are handled through Calendly, a third-party scheduling service. When you book an appointment, you are subject to Calendly's Privacy Policy. We do not store your booking details on our own servers.

5. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects anonymous information such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Approximate location (city level only)
  • Device type (mobile or desktop)
  • How you found the site (search, direct, WhatsApp link)

This data is fully anonymous — we cannot identify individual visitors. No personally identifiable information is collected by Google Analytics. You can opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6. Payments

This website has no payment system. We do not accept, process or store any payment information online. Do not make any online payment to anyone claiming to represent this clinic without first confirming directly with Dr. Sumanth Kumar via our website enquiry form.

7. Third party links

Our website contains links to Google Maps, Google Reviews, WhatsApp and Calendly. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to read their respective privacy policies.

8. Children's privacy

This website does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Our services are provided to patients of all ages, but online interactions are expected to be conducted by adults or guardians.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
